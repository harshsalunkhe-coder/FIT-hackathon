Small clinics often manage appointments using phone calls, notebooks, WhatsApp messages, or spreadsheets. At the same time, patients and their families may face difficulty finding information about ambulance services, blood availability, and nearby healthcare facilities during urgent situations.

Build a software-based healthcare management solution that helps organize patient appointments, basic patient information, ambulance requests, and blood-related requirements in one platform.

The system should allow clinic staff or users to manage basic patient information, schedule appointments, view upcoming appointments, update appointment status, submit ambulance requests, and search or report blood requirements based on blood group and location.

Important: The application should focus on administrative, appointment, emergency coordination, ambulance, and blood-bank information management. It should not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or other medical decision-making.

💡 Suggested Features
Participants may implement features such as:

👤 Patient Registration

📅 Appointment Booking

🕐 Appointment Scheduling

📋 Appointment Status

🔔 Follow-up Reminders

📊 Clinic Dashboard

🗂️ Basic Visit History

🔎 Patient Search

🚑 Ambulance Request

📍 Ambulance Availability / Location

🩸 Blood Group Search

🏥 Blood Bank Information

📍 Nearby Hospital / Clinic Information

🚨 Emergency Request Management

📈 Basic Appointment & Emergency Statistics


🎯 Objective
Create a practical digital solution that reduces manual appointment management and helps users and small healthcare facilities organize appointments and basic emergency coordination more efficiently.

The solution should demonstrate how software can bring important healthcare coordination information such as appointments, ambulance requests, blood requirements, and healthcare facility information together in one accessible platform.

⏱️ Development Scope
This is a software-only challenge.

Focus on the core appointment and emergency coordination workflows rather than attempting to build a complete hospital management system.

🚀 Deployment Requirement
Deploy your working application using Google Cloud Run.

The deployed application must be accessible to the jury.
