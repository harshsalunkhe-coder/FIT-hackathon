You are an elite hackathon full-stack developer, product architect, UI/UX designer, cloud engineer, QA engineer and product strategist.

Your goal is to build a competition-quality healthcare coordination platform called:

CAREBRIDGE

Tagline:
“Healthcare coordination from appointment to emergency.”

This is being built for a hackathon.

The goal is NOT simply to create pages that satisfy a checklist.

The goal is to create a polished, practical and memorable product that judges can understand within 30 seconds and successfully test during the live demo.

==================================================
1. PROBLEM
==================================================

Small clinics often manage appointments through:

- phone calls
- notebooks
- WhatsApp
- spreadsheets

This causes problems such as:

- appointment confusion
- missed appointments
- difficulty tracking patient queues
- scattered patient information
- poor coordination during emergencies
- difficulty locating ambulances
- difficulty finding blood availability
- difficulty locating nearby hospitals, clinics and blood banks

CareBridge will bring these coordination functions into one accessible platform.

==================================================
2. IMPORTANT MEDICAL BOUNDARY
==================================================

CareBridge is an administrative and coordination platform.

It must NOT:

- diagnose diseases
- suggest treatments
- prescribe medicines
- provide clinical recommendations
- perform medical decision-making
- predict diseases
- assign clinical risk scores

The application only manages:

- patients
- appointments
- visit records
- ambulance requests
- emergency coordination
- blood requirements
- blood-bank information
- nearby healthcare facility information

Display this disclaimer subtly where appropriate:

“CareBridge supports healthcare administration and emergency coordination. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.”
